# Adversarial Grounding Review

The first substantive grounding result and the completed Grounding
Retrospective are frozen.

Do not revise either one.

Do not repair, continue, mutate, or supplement the underlying work.

Use only the qualified material and Tooling outputs available to the grounding
exercise.

Act as an independent reviewer attempting to falsify the material conclusions
already reached.

Do not assume that a defect exists.
Do not assume that the exercise succeeded.

## 1. Material Claims

Identify the material claims made by the frozen result and retrospective.

For each claim:

- identify its supporting evidence;
- classify the support as direct, inferential, or unresolved;
- determine whether the claim remains justified at the confidence expressed.

Focus on claims that materially affect the bounded task.

## 2. Assumption Review

Identify any material assumption required for the existing conclusions to hold.

For each:

- state the assumption;
- identify what supports it;
- determine whether the conclusion changes materially if the assumption is
  false.

Do not manufacture hypothetical assumptions that have no practical effect.

## 3. Authority Review

Re-evaluate the reconstructed authority and responsibility boundaries from the
qualified material.

Determine whether every material authority conclusion is adequately supported.

If an alternative interpretation exists, describe it and compare its support.

## 4. Guidance Review

Re-evaluate the guidance treated as applicable.

For every materially influential Process, Policy, instruction, or relation:

- identify the evidence supporting its use;
- determine whether the interpretation given to it was necessary;
- identify any equally supported alternative interpretation.

Do not assume that availability implies applicability or that applicability
implies any particular execution state.

## 5. State Review

Attempt to construct the strongest materially different interpretation of the
bounded current state that is still compatible with the qualified material.

Then classify that alternative as:

- equally supported;
- less supported;
- unsupported;
- unresolved.

Explain the evidence that discriminates between interpretations.

## 6. Source Review

Attempt to identify any material conclusion that depended on information not
qualified by the supplied material or Tooling.

For each candidate:

- identify the dependency;
- determine whether it actually affected the result;
- state whether it represents a reproducibility concern.

If none exist, state that explicitly.

## 7. Tooling-Path Review

Review how Tooling was used during grounding.

Determine whether any material conclusion depended on:

- bypassing a qualified Tooling path;
- substituting manual reconstruction for available structured output;
- treating storage layout or naming as semantic authority;
- continuing after a Tooling failure without equivalent qualification.

Only report cases that actually occurred or remain genuinely uncertain.

## 8. Reproducibility Challenge

Consider a fresh qualified recipient using the same material and Tooling.

Identify the most plausible materially different conclusions such a recipient
could reach.

For each:

- explain why the variation is plausible;
- identify the evidence that should constrain it;
- determine whether the variation indicates ordinary judgment or a durable
  grounding weakness.

Do not assume variation is undesirable.

## 9. Confidence Review

Give separate confidence for each material dimension of the grounding result.

Choose the dimensions based on what actually mattered to the bounded task.

For every dimension below High confidence, explain the exact uncertainty.

Do not introduce dimensions merely because this review asks for them.

## 10. Smallest Discriminating Test

If material uncertainty remains, identify the smallest non-repairing test or
observation that would most efficiently reduce it.

Do not execute the test.

If no additional test is warranted, state why.

## 11. Adversarial Disposition

State:

- which material conclusions survived review;
- which did not;
- what remains unresolved;
- whether any remaining uncertainty should affect the next governing decision;
- your confidence in this assessment.

Do not assume that discrepancies exist.
Do not make a final acceptance claim unless separately authorized.

Do not repair anything.

This review is an adversarial diagnostic of the grounding interpretation only.
