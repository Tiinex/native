# Qualify Portable Session Grounding And Continuity Process

## Objective

Establish one portable Native process for cold-start/resume grounding and checkpoint continuity that remains usable without Tiinex-specific Roles, Business Projects, or host-specific behavior.

## Done Criteria

- Native owns a readable registered `.topics/.processes` discovery surface and one portable Session Grounding And Continuity process offered for reusable package composition.
- The process works for a minimal or role-less Workspace and does not require Anchor, Sigma, the Tiinex Business graph, ChatGPT, GitHub, or another host/provider.
- Process applicability is explicit and is not inferred from catalog presence, carriage, filenames, directories, or Role inventory.
- Carried qualified material is preferred before immutable recovery and live external connector access.
- Host-specific adaptation is delegated to Interop/host ownership and Core remains host-neutral mechanics.
- Artifact identity, Parent lineage, filename/dimension, and carrier continuity remain distinct.
- Checkpoint frequency is based on meaningful progress and survivability risk rather than message count.
- Current Core Entry discovery is confirmed capable of consuming qualified carried Entry-family artifacts without a runtime package dependency on Native; first-party Entry relocation itself remains a later extraction after that one-way seam is proven in the intended bootstrap/carrier path.
- No new schema, JSON support file, dot-prefixed structural namespace, or remote mutation is introduced for this process.

## Scope

Native process catalog and portable process content plus read-only qualification of the existing carried-content import seam. Business applicability/Role policy, OpenAI/ChatGPT adaptation, Core grounding mechanics, actual Entry/schema relocation, bootstrap embedding policy, and remote publication are separate work.

## Dependencies

- Controlling Business Project: Grounding And Continuity Readiness at immutable Business commit `9fc62b5fa91aab00731d4a4f67ac53f495ece38d`.
- Registered discovery-surface convention: Native reusable package guidance may be offered through `.topics/.processes`; ordinary Workspace-local process material may still use `.topics/processes`, and neither placement establishes applicability.
- Existing Session Entry schema may select grounding material/processes when appropriate; this Task does not require a new schema.
- First-party Entry artifacts currently embedded under Core are transitional Native-extraction debt. Do not move them until external/carried Entry discovery and bootstrap selection work without a Core→Native package dependency or duplicate maintained authority.

## Execution Order

1. Establish the Native-local process catalog root without manufacturing unrelated semantic ancestry.
2. Author the portable Session Grounding And Continuity process beneath that root.
3. Qualify the process against current Native structural conventions and shared audit.
4. Update Native human/LLM routing surfaces only as needed to make the process discoverable.
